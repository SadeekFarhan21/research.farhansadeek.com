RESEARCH
========

Learnable Path-Complex Features and Unified Path-Cellular Message Passing for
Link Prediction
  Authors:      Farhan Sadeek, Sie Hendrata Dharmawan, and Peter Chin
  Published:    May 22, 2026
  Project page: https://pcn.farhansadeek.com/
  Paper:        https://openreview.net/pdf?id=n9ORNG6Ay8

  We introduce position-aware learnable initialization for path-complex cells
  and a unified path-cellular complex for link prediction, combining path- and
  ring-based message passing.


Simultaneous Illumination Normalization and Sparse Image Compression via
Unfolded Recovery
  Authors:      Farhan Sadeek
  Published:    May 24, 2026
  Project page: https://snr.farhansadeek.com/
  Paper:        https://openreview.net/pdf?id=KTNxm4pCZg

  The joint inverse-problem pipeline combines illumination normalization and
  sparse image compression, outperforming sequential recovery by up to 4.7 dB
  on textured scenes.
